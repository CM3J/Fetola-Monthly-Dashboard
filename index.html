Part 02

Strategic Event & Paid Media Review

User behavior, channels, and paid media strategy

05

Events hub vs. specific campaigns

✕

Same pattern holds a second month running — verified directly against the July export.

Events hub performance

The generic /events/ directory is still dead weight

The bare /events/ hub page pulled just 6 sessions in July (up slightly from 1 in June, still negligible). Visitors don't browse a calendar listing organically — they land on the specific event page they were pointed to, or they don't land at all.

Direct webinar targeting

Specific event pages keep working — but rotate fast

June's top event page (/start-with-what-you-have/, 182 sessions) dropped to just 2 sessions in July, replaced almost exactly by a new one: /turn-your-impact-into-growth/ pulled 404 sessions on its own. The tactic works — but each specific event page has a short shelf life and needs a fresh one lined up monthly.

06

GA4 user-count questions

✕
Why the numbers look off

"New + Returning don't add up"

This is expected, not a tracking error. Total Users (804) is deduplicated once, site-wide. But a user who visits via two different channels in the same period appears in both of those channels' individual totals. That's why the per-channel numbers sum to about 819 while the actual site total is 804 — a 15-user overlap from cross-channel visits, not missing or double-counted data.

SEO discovery timing

Zero new users from Organic Search

Short-lived webinar pages generally don't have time to get indexed and rank in Google before the event has already passed — so it's expected that these pages lean on paid and direct traffic rather than organic discovery. We're pulling the New-Users-by-channel breakdown to confirm the exact figure for next review.

07

Paid media for events

✕

Google Ads delivered 428 sessions and 286 users in July at strong 67.3% engagement — consistent with June, though the traffic still mostly lands on the Homepage and Programmes page rather than specific event pages. We still don't have equivalent access to Paid Social spend and performance data, so we can't fairly compare cost-effectiveness between the two channels for events yet — that's still the next data pull.

08

Immediate tactical recommendations

✕
🔗

1. Link the funnel top-of-page

The single highest-leverage fix: add a visible sign-up/contact CTA directly on /turn-your-impact-into-growth/, the homepage, and /nedbank-fashion-accelerator-programme/ — July's three highest-traffic pages sent 0 visitors to the conversion pages.

↔️

2. Retarget warm audiences

Set up Meta and LinkedIn pixels to target the ~1,339 users from July who visited but didn't convert. Cheaper than fresh acquisition, and they already know the brand.

🎯

3. Coordinate webinar pathing

Route all future promotional spend directly to the specific event landing page, bypassing the generic /events/ hub entirely — coordinate with Mihlali Baliya on which webinar to point to next, given July's page is already rotating out.

📈

4. Shift focus to action metrics

Judge campaign performance on conversions, not raw session volume alone — July is the sharpest case study yet: sessions up 65%, conversions down to zero.
